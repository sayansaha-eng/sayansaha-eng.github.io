Drone Propeller Optimization

Goal

To perform a data-driven design optimization focused on maximizing lift while minimizing the acoustic noise of drone propellers.

Approach

  • Established optimization loops utilizing Principal Component Analysis (PCA).
  • Applied Bayesian optimization to refine parameters iteratively.
  • Prototyped iterations using 3D-printing technologies.

Results

Achieved a quantifiable reduction in acoustic noise signatures while maintaining required thrust margins.
